# Cobalt BOMTOON Context

Cobalt provides a Kobo-native reader for a BOMTOON account. This glossary distinguishes wallet assets from episode-access entitlements so the application never claims that one asset can perform another asset's job.

## Wallet assets

**Coin**:
A spendable balance acquired with real money. Coin can pay for an episode rental or permanent episode purchase.
_Avoid_: Paid coin, ticket

**Bonus Coin**:
A spendable promotional balance awarded with a real-money coin top-up.
_Avoid_: Gift, free coin

**Free Coin**:
A spendable promotional balance awarded through events or obtained by redeeming a Ticket.
_Avoid_: Gift, bonus coin

**Spendable Coin Balance**:
The combined Coin, Bonus Coin, and Free Coin available for episode commerce. BOMTOON, not the reader, determines which bucket a charge consumes.
_Avoid_: Coin type choice

**Ticket**:
A coupon that can discount a real-money coin top-up or be redeemed into Free Coin. A Ticket does not directly rent or purchase an episode.
_Avoid_: Episode ticket, rental coupon, gift

**Mileage**:
Loyalty points exchangeable into Coin at 200 Mileage per Coin once at least 3,000 Mileage is available.
_Avoid_: Coin, ticket

## Episode access

**Gift**:
A coupon consumed to obtain a specific episode rental without spending Coin.
_Avoid_: Ticket, free coin

**Episode Rental**:
Time-limited access to an episode, obtained by spending Coin or consuming a Gift.
_Avoid_: Purchase, ownership

**Episode Purchase**:
Permanent access to an episode obtained by spending Coin.
_Avoid_: Coin purchase, possession rental

**Coin Top-up**:
Acquiring Coin with real money. It is separate from spending Coin on an episode and is outside the episode-commerce flow.
_Avoid_: Episode purchase

**Gift Redemption**:
Consuming a Gift to create an Episode Rental.
_Avoid_: Ticket redemption, free rental

**Ticket Redemption**:
Consuming a Ticket to obtain Free Coin. It is separate from episode commerce.
_Avoid_: Gift redemption, episode purchase
